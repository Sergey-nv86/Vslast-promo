## Licensing rule

Only assets whose source page explicitly permits commercial use without mandatory attribution may be placed here.

Preferred sources:
- Pixabay Content License: https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/
- UNSTUNTED SFX: https://unstuntedsfx.net/ — its bread collection states that the sounds are cleared for worldwide commercial use without attribution.

Do not add:
- CC BY-NC
- attribution-required assets
- assets with unclear commercial rights
- assets distributed only for personal/non-commercial use
